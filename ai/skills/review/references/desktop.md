# Desktop and native UI

Product and flow review for native window and control chrome. Trace the affected surface through the broader experience.

## Interaction and product fit

- Whether the change matches the app’s established interaction language (spare utility chrome, quiet depth, native-control-first) or introduces a second product aesthetic across features.
- Dismiss, activation, and multi-display behavior coherent with other floating or main windows in the product.
- Lists, tables, and shared row chrome that stay consistent for selection, hints, and secondary labels across the flow.

## Keyboard, focus, and accessibility flows

- Tab order, focus restoration after dismiss or view swap, and overlay containment for VoiceOver across the affected flow — not only one control’s label.
- Material status and selection changes announced or otherwise exposed beyond paint alone; color not the sole cue across the flow.
- Reduced-motion and theme-aware state that still works for the whole surface under review.
